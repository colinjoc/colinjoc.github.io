title: "Principles Developed From Working With AI"
---

## 1. Conciseness is what's valuable

Text is cheap. Meaning is expensive.

When I talk to an AI, or when an AI talks for me, the goal is to convey meaning in as few words as possible — as clearly as possible.

## 2. Define success first

All problems are computable. The question is whether you've defined what "correct" looks like.

Define success first. A test is just the runnable form of "I'll know it when it passes." The medium doesn't matter — code, AI, or a team. Once success is defined, the answer is just a search problem.
