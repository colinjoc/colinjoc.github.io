## Surprising Results

**[The night-time wet-bulb hypothesis does not survive a pre-registered test on 9,276 city-weeks](/hdr/results/heat-mortality/)** — The Vecellio (2022) wet-bulb threshold revision, widely cited in the Lancet Countdown, is refuted across 10 robustness specifications on 30 US and EU cities. Night-time wet-bulb adds zero predictive information beyond dry-bulb temperature.

**[Four smart cars dissolve a phantom traffic jam](/hdr/results/phantom-jams/)** — On the Sugiyama ring road, 4 out of 22 vehicles (18.2%) running Adaptive Cruise Control suppress stop-and-go waves by 93.3%. The transition is sharp: 3 vehicles still leave a 1.77 m/s wave; 4 vehicles drop it to 0.55 m/s.

**[120 pre-registered ML experiments could not beat the baseline — until we looked at per-stage timestamps](/hdr/results/building-permits/)** — Generic feature engineering saturates at 89-day MAE on cross-city building permit data. Seattle's per-correction-letter timestamps drop it to 25 days. The answer was structural, not algorithmic.
